**2026-11-17 · Track W · V4f — the contrast check of a motion ad's frames before the last**

Built — `creative/motion/motionFrames.ts`:

- `checkMotionFrames`: the moving picture, with our scrim, band and logo over it and no word, is sampled every
  half second and on the motion's last frame. Under every line that has arrived by then, the pixels go to the
  still's own judge (`checkRender`), at the still's line boxes and sample step. A line that fails on any sampled
  frame is one finding: the line, the first moment, the lowest ratio, how many frames. A finding refuses the
  motion ad; the still is untouched.
- The frames are made by the join's own picture chain (`motionPictureChain`, now one definition read by both), so
  what is judged is what is shown.
- `renderMotionLayers` also answers `ground` (scrim, band and logo, before any word) and `lines` (each row's
  line boxes). `under` could not be sampled: it carries the phone and the equal-opportunity line, and a line's own
  ink is not its background. For the same reason the joined file itself is not what is sampled.

Measured (2026-11-17), and it changes what this check is for:

- With a dark ground colour and white words, the weakest scrim step (45 %) leaves white at about 3.3:1 over a frame
  that is white edge to edge, and every row of today's three looks is large text, which needs 3:1. So for such a
  kit **no picture can fail this check** — the scrim's floor is the protection. The check decides for a kit with no
  ground colour (`plate_color` null), a lighter ground, or words that are not white.
- A clip shorter than was asked does not end early: the picture chain holds its last frame. Three seconds where
  five were asked is two seconds of a frozen picture. Nothing refuses that yet; the stored clip's length is on its
  ledger row (V4e).

Ours, with nothing published behind it: a sample every half second (half of the shortest time a group of words is
given). Something bright that crosses a line between two samples is not seen.

Not built: anything that calls it. It runs when a motion ad is composed (the `video` kind, next).
