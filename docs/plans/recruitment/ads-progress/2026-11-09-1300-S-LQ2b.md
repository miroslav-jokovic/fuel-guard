**2026-11-09 · Track S · LQ2b every ad lead has one owner (this PR)**

- **Built:**
  - `POST /api/recruitment/openings/:id/recruiter` (recruitment manage, audited
    `recruitment.opening_recruiter_changed`). It refuses `not_a_recruiter` (422) unless the person holds
    recruitment manage today, per the access matrix (`sectionManagerIds`); null hands the opening back to
    everyone.
  - `GET /api/recruitment/recruiters` (recruitment view): the matrix's recruitment managers, by name or else
    email.
  - `listOpenings` returns `recruiter_id`.
  - New `leads/leadAssignment.ts` `assignFromOpening`, registered in `scripts/table-writers.json`. A NEW ad lead
    for an opening whose recruiter still holds recruitment manage is written `assigned_to` (`is assigned_to
    null`, so an owned lead is never taken back).
  - `ringNewLead` rings only that owner ("New lead for your opening"). With no owner it rings the list or the
    managers, as before. `lead_uncontacted` still escalates to the whole list.
  - An assignment that throws is logged `[lead-assign]` and the lead rings everyone. A throw would have failed
    the accept, the retry would read the lead as a duplicate, and nobody would have been rung.
  - Web: a Recruiter column on Recruitment › Openings. Managers get a dropdown (Everyone in recruiting +
    managers); viewers get the name as text. A recruiter who lost access shows as "Someone without recruiting
    access", never as everyone's.
- **Not built + why:**
  - A typed-in lead is not assigned: the ruling was about ad leads arriving unwatched, and who owns a typed-in
    lead was never asked.
  - The lead queue's "Mine" filter needs `recruiting_lead_queue` (an RPC, keyset-paged) to take an assignee. A
    client-side filter over one page would be incomplete, so it is a migration plus reader of its own (LQ2c).
- **Mutants:** 8. Seven were killed: save without the matrix check, assign without the matrix check, owned lead
  taken back, ring to everyone, assignment throwing, list without recruiter_id, no "lost access" option. M8
  (empty string vs null) was a no-op: AppSelect answers the Everyone option as null, so the dead guard was
  removed.
