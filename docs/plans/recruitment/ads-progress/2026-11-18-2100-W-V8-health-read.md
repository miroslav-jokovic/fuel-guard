  (`{META_APP_ID}|{META_APP_SECRET}`), the form the page requires; the user token rides as `input_token` inside the
  client (`MetaGraph.inspectOwnToken`) and is scrubbed from any error. Read at every Connect and Test, and once a
  day by the reconcile job (`connections/tokenWatch.ts`), written to `token_expires_on` and the last report's
  `token_expiry`. Admins get `ads_token_expiring` once a day from 14 days before it lapses (critical from 3 days, and
  the day Meta calls it invalid). A new setting, **`META_APP_ID`**, joins the owner's credential list; unset, the
  expiry is reported "not read" with that reason.
