**2026-11-16 · Track W · V3f — each ad as a Facebook feed post, and what Meta's own pages say**

Read first (Meta's Ads Guide, 2026-11-16): Facebook feed image ad — ratio 4:5, primary text 50–150 characters,
headline 27; Instagram Stories image ad — primary text 125, keep about 14 % of the top, 35 % of the bottom and 6 %
of each side clear. Recommendations, not limits. Neither page says where a long text is cut, nor how a 4:5 image is
shown in Stories.

Built:

- **One rule for what Meta is handed** (`shared/ads/platformText.ts`): the two texts beside the image
  (`platformText`) and the image a feed ad shows (`feedImageOf`). The publisher's `linkData` and `creativeRefusal`
  now use them, and so does the review screen, so a preview cannot show words the publisher would not send.
- **The campaign read** hands each ad its two texts per language (`platform_text`), by the slots the ad's own
  preset version names; one read of those versions, in this carrier's rows.
- **The feed frame** (`AdsFeedPreview.vue`), under each language on the review screen: the text above the picture,
  the picture, the headline under it, the button. It says it is a drawing and not Meta's own preview; the whole
  text is shown; a text past Meta's recommendation gets a sentence with its count (`feedTextNotes`).
- **No story frame**, on purpose: we send the feed image only (Q-AD29), and how Meta shows it in Stories is not
  documented. Q-APX11.

Measured, and asked of the owner as Q-APX10: of the Copywriter's 360 recorded texts above the picture per language,
59 % in English and 72 % in Spanish run past Meta's 150 characters; the headlines are fine (median 23).

Looked at in a browser at 1280 and 390 px, English (inside the recommendations) and Spanish (past both).
