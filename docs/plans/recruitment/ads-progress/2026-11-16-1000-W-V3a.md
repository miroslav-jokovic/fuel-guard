**2026-11-16 · Track W · V3a — the photo library, and what "make many, show few" costs**

Built:

- **A kit photo says what it shows.** `equipment_photos[].tags`, any of truck, trailer, driver, yard, road
  (`AD_PHOTO_TAGS`). In the kit's versioned JSON: a version saved before the tags parses with none, the next save
  writes what was ticked. No migration.
- **A kit with one photo is told it will repeat itself (F-AG5)** — in the kit editor under the photos, and on the
  kit's row in Settings › Ads control, which now also reads "3 photos · 2 truck, 1 yard". A kit with no photo is
  told an equipment ad cannot be made; photos with nothing ticked are counted. One pure function
  (`photoLibraryAdvice`) for both places.
- **Nothing reads the tags yet.** The Art director is not told them in this step: telling it is a prompt change
  and a recording, and it goes with the change that makes one ad's three pictures differ (Q-APX8), one recording
  for both.

Measured (fixture data, the owner's key, no fixture rewritten), in the plan as Q-APX7:

- One call of each worker in tokens and dollars; an ad costs $0.34 in model calls today and $0.88 with three
  pictures, three sets of words and nine pairings judged in the composed frame.
- The finished-ad judge asked four times about each of 16 ads: a total never moved by more than 1 point of 20;
  sound ads 16 to 18.25, planted-weak ads 10 to 15. A 1-point gap between two ads is the judge's own movement.

Asked of the owner: Q-APX7 (how many, which pairings are judged), Q-APX8 (what makes the pictures differ),
Q-APX9 (what is kept of the rivals) — each with a recommendation.

Not done: several pictures per ad, the pairing rank, the review screen's rivals, the preview frames, the reference
set. The measuring script is not in the repository; it called `EVAL_WORKERS` requests through `callForcedTool`.
