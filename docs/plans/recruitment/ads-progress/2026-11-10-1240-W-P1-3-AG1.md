**The ceiling is measured, and D-AD27 is corrected by it.** The plan said "the most characters that can fit two
lines at the narrowest plausible advance". Computed that way (the mean advance of a–z and a space) it is 50 on 4:5
and 52 on 1:1 — and it is wrong: of the 360 headlines in the copywriter's recorded fixtures that reach the layout
step (370 recorded; 10 are refused by the claim check first), 9 of 10 between 50 and 54 characters fit. Measured
with the real overlay instead: everything of 45 or fewer fits both formats; nothing over 56 fits either; between
them it depends on the words. So the ceiling is 56, the longest that fit, and `overlayCapacity.test.ts` re-measures
it on every published format and layout — a template size, a safe zone, the face or a re-record that moves it
turns that file red, and the figure is then measured again, never adjusted to pass. The shared contract fixture's
`hook: 60` became 56.
