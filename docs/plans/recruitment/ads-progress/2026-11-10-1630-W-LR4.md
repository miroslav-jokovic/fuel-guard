- Built:
  - `recruiting/leads/leadQuality.ts` reads the closures of the window's leads in `seq` order and counts a lead
    under a lost reason only while that close is its NEWEST act (reopened = open; closed twice = the later
    reason, once). Every `LEAD_LOST_REASONS` key is in the answer, zero included. A `not_qualified:*` close is
    not counted here: it already is `not_qualified` through its verified verdict.
  - Shared `LeadQualityCounts.closed` is a `Record<string, number>`: the ads side carries the lead owner's
    vocabulary, it does not name it (D-AD17; nothing in `packages/shared/src/ads` imports recruiting).
  - `analystBrief.ts`: the fold takes `closed` (defaulted `{}`; keys must be plain lower-case words, counts
    ≥ 0); the sheet gains `closed_by_reason` with only the reasons that have a lead, keyed in words; the plain
    brief appends "closed by a recruiter for another reason: 3 not interested, 11 out of area".
  - Analyst `prompt.v6.md` (`analyst@6`): the field described, and rule 7 says how to word it and that it is
    neither "missed a hard requirement" nor the cohorts' count.
