**2026-11-16 · Track W · V4c — ffmpeg on the server, and the join that makes a motion ad's file**

The owner ruled Q-APX7 to Q-APX15 as proposed ("proceed as proposed … high grade and really good quality, modern and
in trending"); the rulings are in the plan's §5a.

Built:

- **ffmpeg** in `infra/image/Dockerfile` (`app` target only, Debian's package; the build fails unless libx264 and
  aac are among its encoders) and in CI's `test-api` job.
- **`joinMotion`** (`creative/motion/motionJoin.ts`): the model's clip + `renderMotionLayers`' layers + the still →
  one MP4. The clip is covered to 1080 × 1350 at 30 frames a second and cut to the timeline's length; the first words
  are on frame 0; a later group fades in and rises 24 px over 0.3 s from its cue and is not drawn before it; the
  motion's last frame dissolves over 0.4 s into the approved still, which is held. The clip's own sound is never
  mapped; the track is silence.
- **Refusals by name:** `ffmpeg_missing`, `ffmpeg_failed` (with ffmpeg's last words), `ffmpeg_timeout`, a timeline
  made for other layers, a hold too short for the dissolve.

Measured while building:

- **Encoding the silence in the same run moved the picture.** With edit lists off, the AAC encoder's first packet
  (1,024 samples before zero) made ffmpeg hold the first frame 0.0547 s against 0.0333 s for every other. The
  silence is now encoded to its own file first and copied in: every frame is the same length.
- The fixture's 7-second file is 1.2 MB and joins in about a second on the laptop. The worker's time is not
  measured; the size the image grows by is not measured either (no Docker running here) — `image.yml` builds it on
  this PR.

What the tests read from the file itself: codec, size, pixel format, frame count, equal frame times, one silent
stereo AAC track though the clip carried a tone, no `elst`, `moov` before `mdat`, the last frame equal to the
still, the supporting line absent before its cue and whole after it. In CI a run without ffmpeg fails rather than
skips.

Not built: the clip request (Q-APX12), the check of the frames before the last, the publisher's video road, the
`video` kind in `CAMPAIGN_WORKERS`, the review screen's player.

Mutants: 14 planted one at a time, 14 caught.

Traps: zsh does not split `$V` into words (use `bash -c` for a loop over option strings); a seek to the last
frame's time can return nothing — pick a frame by its number.
