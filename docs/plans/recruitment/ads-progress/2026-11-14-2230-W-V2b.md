**2026-11-14 · Track W · V2b — what a plan is, and the machine's planning moves**

Built:

- **The plan contract** (`packages/shared/src/ads/campaignPlan.ts`). Evidence is items with an id and a kind
  (`job`, `our_result`, `lead_outcome`, `benchmark`, `swipe`, `market`). A plan is who the ad speaks to and three
  to six angles; an angle names its hook and scene from the lists the presets already use, a format, an idea, a
  proof, a reason, and the evidence ids it rests on. `checkPlan` refuses a plan whole on any of: an id the pack
  does not hold; an angle citing no fact of the job; two angles with the same hook and scene; a format no worker
  can make (only `still` is built); a figure or a promise in the plan's own words that the opening does not hold —
  read by the same `checkClaims` the copy is read by, the audience line included. `jobEvidence` turns a brief into
  evidence items with stable ids.
- **The machine's planning moves** (`campaignMachine.ts`): `draft → plan → planning`, `plan_ready → plan_review`,
  `plan_failed → failed`, `plan_approved → composing` and `replan → planning` by a person only,
  `failed → resume_plan`. `CAMPAIGN_STATES_AWAITING_ENTRY` is empty again, as V2a's test required.
- **The plan is approved by name** (`transitions.ts`): `plan_approved` writes `plan_id` in the same
  compare-and-set as the state, and is refused and recorded (`plan_not_named`) without one, before the database's
  AX032 would refuse it. No other move writes a plan id.
- **The runner** takes `plan` from a draft when this host has a planner and `compose` when it has none; in
  `planning` it runs the planner and ends in `plan_ready`, `plan_failed`, or waits. Under the brake nothing is
  planned.

**No host plans yet.** `CampaignWorkers.plan` is `null` in `adsWorkers`: the plan store and the Strategist are
V2c. So no campaign's road changed with this merge, and every golden scenario is as it was.

**`draft → compose` stays in the table**, as the road for a host with no planner. Both roads are rows of the
machine and the runner chooses between them by one fact (is there a planner); it is removed in the step that
makes planning unconditional, which needs the Strategist certified first.
