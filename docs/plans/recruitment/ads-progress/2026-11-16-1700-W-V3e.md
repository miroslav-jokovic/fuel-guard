**2026-11-16 · Track W · V3e — the review screen shows what an ad was chosen from, and a person can use another**

This meets the first half of V3's "done when": the review screen shows the winner with its rivals.

Built:

- **The rivals on the review screen** (`AdsPairingRivals.vue`), under the checks, for an ad chosen from several
  pictures: every pairing best first with its image, picture number, copy number and score of 20; the one that is
  the ad marked; a pairing within one point of the best called **"As good"**, with the reason under the grid — a
  one-point gap is never shown as "better" or "second" (Q-APX7's measurement); a picture that was made and not
  used, with compose's reason; "Not ranked" when the judge did not run. What it says about a score is one shared
  function (`adRivals`).
- **Using another one.** "Use this one" → a confirmation naming the picture and the copy, saying the picture is
  not made again → the same door as "Use these words" (`chooseWords`), now with `picture`. The ad shown is
  rejected with the reason, and the new ad is built on the picture named, replayed from its ledger rows.
- **The door refuses a picture the ad cannot use** (`unknown_picture`): one never made, one that was dropped, any
  picture of an ad made from one. The same words on a different picture is a different choice
  (`chosen_differently`).
- **Links.** The campaign read signs the one image kept of each pairing, in the same call as the ad's own images
  and under the same folder rule.

Looked at in a browser (local build, fake session, stubbed API, real fixture images): ranked, unranked and
one-picture ads at 1280 px, and the ranked one at 390 px. Two things found by looking: the score wrapped
mid-phrase ("17 of / 20"), now on its own line; and the mark on the picked pairing never showed — `picking === r`
compared Vue's proxy with the object — now compared by picture and words, with a badge.

Mutants: 13 planted one at a time, 13 caught.

Not done in V3: the preview frames (Facebook feed, Instagram story, both languages, Meta's safe zones — which the
plan says are measured on the sandbox, and there is no sandbox account yet), and the carrier's reference set
(Q-APX3 ruled it is made from ordinary approvals and rejections, 50 labels; there are none yet).
