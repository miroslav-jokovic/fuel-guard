**2026-11-19 · Track W · V6's workspace screens (plan §0.1; Q-APX18 (a))**

- **Ads is its own sidebar section**, after Recruitment, with one entry (`ads.overview`, `/ads`) and its tabs:
  Overview, Campaigns, Creative studio, Automation and Settings. The tab list is built from the catalogue
  (`adsWorkspace.ts`) and filtered by `canReachSurface` and `surfaceAllowed` (`useAdsTabs.ts`), so a role sees only
  the tabs its grants open.
  - `recruitment.campaigns` is gone; production held 0 overrides on it. `admin.recruiting.ads` keeps its key and
    moves only its path to `/ads/settings`, because the fleet switched it on for recruiters on 2026-11-05 (one
    `org_role_surface_access` row) and a new key would have taken it away from them without anyone noticing.
  - `/recruitment/campaigns` (keeping `?campaign=`) and `/settings/recruiting/ads` redirect. Leads and Openings stay in
    Recruitment.
- **Overview** draws `GET /api/ads/overview`: four figures, "Needs you" with a link to each campaign, and the action
  history paged by cursor with the shared log pager. Its leads are Meta's count, and the page says so.
- **Automation** draws `GET /api/ads/health` (V8's page).
- **Creative studio** holds the brand kit and the presets (moved off Settings) and the **swipe file**: each saved page
  or ad picture with its state, the facts kept from it with their quotes, what the check left out and why, and the
  doors to save a page, save a picture, read again and archive. Reading is a view of recruiting; the doors are a
  manage grant, asked as `session.can("recruitment")`, the API's `requireSection("recruitment", "manage")`. The list
  is read again every 5 s while an entry is being read, and not otherwise. A picture goes up the brand kit's way
  (`putFile` is now shared rather than copied).
- **`isWorking` is derived.** `CAMPAIGN_STATES_AGENT_WORKING` (shared `adsOverview.ts`) is a state whose every
  forward step an agent may take — kill, archive and pause are not forward steps, so `live` is not "working". It is
  the mirror of `CAMPAIGN_STATES_AWAITING_PERSON`, and a test holds the two disjoint. The web's hand list is gone.
- `adsLabels.ts` holds `PLATFORM_LABEL` and `AGENT_LABEL` once; the settings form and the spend card each had a copy.
- Mutations, each alone, each red: no surface check on the tabs, the redirect dropping its query, the Settings link
  shown to everyone, the machine-derived move words, the Older cursor, `pause` counted as a forward step, the swipe
  doors shown to a viewer, "Read again" offered on an entry being read, and a picture's note left out of the save.
  One survived: a hand-written tab list yields the same tabs on today's catalogue, so no test can tell it apart
  until the catalogue changes. The list is built from the catalogue anyway.

**Not built:** a preview of a saved picture (the list read carries no signed URL; the reading is what the Researcher
