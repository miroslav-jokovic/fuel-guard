**2026-11-16 · Track W · V3d — an ad is chosen from several pictures**

Built, as the recommended answers to Q-APX7 (b) and Q-APX9 (a), ahead of the rulings (the count is a setting):

- **`budgets.pictures_per_creative`**, default 3, 1 to 5 (`MAX_PICTURES_PER_CREATIVE`, the bound migration 0619
  gave `ads_copy_choices.picture`). A settings version saved before this parses with 3.
- **One picture is its own unit** (`compose/creativePictures.ts`, `makePicture`): direction → render → the words
  drawn → the visual check, regenerating on a failed check as before. It returns `passed` with its drawn images, or
  `dropped` with the reason. Each picture is told the first directions of the ones before it.
- **Pairings** (`compose/creativePairings.ts`, `rankPictures`): every passed picture × every set of words it can
  carry, each judged once in the frame the picture was composed for; ranked by `rankPairings` (shared, pure) —
  within one point of the best total the craft judge's order of the words decides, then the order the pictures
  were made in; the winner is then judged in every other format it is shown in.
- **What is stored** (`ads_creatives.pairings`, 0619): the pictures made (a dropped one with why; the words a
  picture could not carry with why), the ranked pairings with scores, and one image per pairing — the English ad
  in the judged frame.
- **The creative stops only when no picture can be used.** One shared reason is said once, as before; different
  reasons are each named with their picture.
- **The judge not running stops nothing** (Q-AD76): nothing is ranked, and the ad is the first picture under the
  best-ranked words. After one refusal the other pairings are not asked.
- **Keys.** Picture 1 keeps every key a creative had before (`art:`, `render:`, `visual:`); later pictures add
  `p<n>:` (`pictureKeyPart`). A pairing's judgement is keyed by picture and by the words' place in the writer's
  order. At one picture the judge's keys are the old ones.
- **The review screen's sentence stays true**: when the ad's words are not the first-ranked because of the
  picture, it says "these words went best with the picture that was chosen", and names the fit only for copies
  that were in fact passed over. The rivals themselves are not shown yet.

At one picture nothing changed: the 101 compose tests from before pass unedited.

Mutants: 19 planted one at a time, 16 caught on the first pass. The three survivors were holes in the tests, and one
was a fault in the design:

- **One unreadable judge answer left eight pairings unranked.** The code stopped asking after ANY call that gave no
  score. Only a switched-off judge or a spent budget would refuse the next call the same way; a failed or
  off-schema answer is that call's alone. The judge now says which (`standing`), and a failed call costs one
  pairing. Test: "one answer the judge's schema refuses leaves that pairing unjudged and last; the other eight are
  still asked and ranked".
- A later picture told a regenerated picture's second direction instead of its first — now asserted.
- The ad's checks taken from picture 1 when another picture won — test "the ad's checks are the winning picture's
  own".

Re-planted with three more: 6 of 6 caught.

Found while reading the word-choice door for the next step, and fixed here: **"Use these words" would have
redrawn the ad on picture 1** when the winning picture was the second or third, although the door's own sentence
says the same picture is kept. The picture kept is now the one the choice names, else the winning pairing's, at
that picture's own attempt (`choiceFor`). Five more mutants on that, five caught.
