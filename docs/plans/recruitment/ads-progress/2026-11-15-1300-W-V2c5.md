**2026-11-15 · Track W · V2c-5 — the plan review screen**

Built (`apps/web/src/features/ads/`): `AdsCampaignPlan.vue`, its rules in `adsPlan.ts`, three hooks in
`useAdsCampaigns.ts`, and the drawer's footer action.

- **Reading.** In the campaign drawer: who the ads speak to, then each idea with what it leads with, what the
  picture shows, the idea, what makes it true and why — and under each, "The facts behind this", which opens the
  evidence it rests on, labelled by where it came from (the job, our earlier ads, our earlier applicants, an
  industry figure). A person approves a plan they can check.
- **Approving** is the drawer's primary action while the plan waits for review, in the footer with the
  campaign's other actions. It names the version on screen.
- **Editing.** A person changes the words and the kind of each idea, and may remove an idea down to the fewest a
  plan may hold. The facts an idea cites are not typed by hand. The contract's own limits mark a field too
  short or too long and hold Save back; the door's refusal is listed in sentences in office words.
- After approval the plan stays on the campaign, read-only. A campaign made before planning existed shows nothing.
- The lists (what an ad can lead with, what a picture can show) and every limit are read from the shared
  contract; nothing is restated on the screen.

**Browser pass** (real build, Chromium, stubbed API; 1280 and 390 wide; read, facts open, edit, a refused edit,
after approval, the plan failing to load, no plan). It found two things the component tests could not:

1. Approve was at the end of the plan — below five ideas, out of sight — while the footer offered only "Write a
   new plan" and "Archive". It moved to the footer.
2. After a refused save the reasons were listed at the top of a long editor while the person was at the Save
   button at the bottom. The list moved beside Save and is scrolled into view when it arrives. Approve is held
   back while an edit is open, so an unsaved edit is never approved around.

Also seen and correct: when the plan fails to load the drawer says so with Retry and offers no Approve (there
is no version to name); no horizontal overflow at either width.

**Not seen with real data.** No server plans yet, so there is no real plan on staging to open. The pass used a
plan the Strategist actually wrote (the recorded `leads-out-of-area` case) over the facts it was written from.
