**2026-11-15 · Track W · V2c-7 — the ads are made from the approved plan**

Until this step a planned and approved campaign made its ads exactly as an unplanned one did, from the seeded
preset draw; the plan was read, edited, approved and stored, and nothing downstream read it.

**What was found first.** An ad is always made from a preset — its copy slots, its picture template, its
layout. A plan's idea names a lead and a picture from the same two lists presets are classified by: twenty-five
possible pairs. A carrier has about five presets. So the planning agent could propose, and a person approve, an
idea that no preset can make.

Built:

- **The planning agent is shown only the kinds of ad the carrier can make** — the lead and picture of each
  enabled preset (`plan/concepts.ts`), each with what it means — in place of two free lists (prompt
  `strategist@3`). A plan naming any other kind is refused (`checkPlan`'s new finding, `concept_not_available`),
  for the agent's plan and a person's edit alike.
- **A planned campaign's ads are the plan's ideas, in the plan's order** (`planDraw`, `compose/campaignDraw`):
  for each idea, the enabled preset with that lead and picture. Stored in the same column and shape as the seeded
  draw, so everything after it is unchanged. A preset already running for the same opening is left out, as before.
  A campaign with no plan draws by seed, as before.
- **One read, three users.** What the agent is offered, what the check allows and what compose draws from are the
  same preset rows, so they cannot differ.
- **Fewer than three kinds of ad enabled:** no plan can pass, so the model is not asked and the campaign stops
  with what to do ("enable more ad presets in Settings, each with its own lead and picture").
- **The review screen** offers a person editing an idea one choice — a kind of ad the carrier can make — in
  place of two free lists, and marks an idea's own kind if it can no longer be made.
- A replayed answer is held to the kinds that were on the sheet it was written from, not the presets enabled
  today (the same rule as the evidence).

**The recording** (said in the session before it ran; fixture data only): `strategist@3` on `claude-fable-5-2`,
the same 8 openings, now each with the kinds of ad its carrier can make (five in most; three for the one-fact
job; four and seven in two others). **8 of 8 on two consecutive passes**; every recorded plan uses only kinds on
its sheet. The same two openings as before needed the second ask in both passes.

**Still not built: the idea's own words do not reach the writer.** The plan now decides WHICH kinds of ad are
made and in what order. Each idea's one-sentence direction ("lead with the lanes and the requirement, plainly")
is not yet handed to the Copywriter or the Art director — they still write from the preset's own formula. That
changes two agents' inputs, so it needs their prompts re-recorded and their evaluations re-run; it is the next
step, on its own.

Mutants: 17 planted one at a time, 16 caught on the first pass; the survivor (matching an idea to a preset by
its lead alone) has a test now — two presets sharing a lead — re-planted and caught.
