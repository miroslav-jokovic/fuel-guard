**2026-11-15 · Track W · V2c-2 — the Strategist, and the planning step that uses it**

Built:

- **The Strategist** (`workers/strategist/`), the seventh Claude agent, on `claude-fable-5-2`. It reads one sheet:
  the evidence pack whole, and the lists an angle chooses from (hooks, scenes, formats that can be made today,
  how many angles) read off the shared contract — its prompt names no hook, scene or format of its own, and a
  test holds that. It answers with the plan through one tool call, and the plan goes through `checkPlan`.
- **One second ask.** A refused plan is asked for once more with the refused plan and each finding as a sentence
  on the sheet. A second refusal fails the step with those sentences.
- **A replayed answer is checked against the pack that was sent.** The planning step can run twice and the pack
  is read from live tables, so on a replay the pack is read back from the ledger row's own request. The plan's
  row keeps the evidence its ids were chosen from.
- **The planning step** (`plan/planCampaign.ts`): pack → Strategist → store. Its failures are sentences an office
  reader can act on (switched off, no budget set, budget spent, refused twice and why).
- **Registered everywhere an agent is**: `AD_AI_AGENTS`, `AD_AGENT_MODELS`, the baseline-model test, the
  Settings switch and its label ("Plans the campaign before any ad is made"), the readiness registry, the
  launch-refusal words, `adsCertifyPlan`. It starts switched OFF in every org, like every agent (D-RA9).

**No host plans yet.** `CampaignWorkers.plan` is still null and the Strategist is not in `CAMPAIGN_WORKERS`: a
campaign moved into `plan_review` today would have no door out. Both change in the merge that adds the approval
and edit routes (V2c-3), which is also where recruiting's `LeadQuality` is handed to the step.

**The recordings** (each said to the owner in the session before it ran; fixture data only): 8 openings on
`claude-fable-5-2` — a carrier with no history, leads from out of area, one concept that clearly won, thin
results, a job with one fact, an instruction inside the carrier's own line, a local job whose leads missed a
requirement, an over-the-road job with many facts.

- **strategist@1: 8 of 8, then 7 of 8.** The miss was an answer outside the plan's shape. The recorded plans
  ran at the contract's limits (an audience of 385 characters against 400, a `why` of 290 against 300, six
  evidence ids against six), so a run in a few put one text over and the whole answer was discarded — and an
  answer outside the shape has no second ask. A single pass would have shipped this.
- **strategist@2** states tighter lengths in the prompt (the contract's limits did not move). **8 of 8 on two
  consecutive passes.** The longest recorded texts are now an audience of 276 characters, a `why` of 188 and
  four evidence ids. Two of the eight openings (the one-fact job and the many-fact job) reached a sound plan
  only on the second ask in every pass, so the second ask is doing real work and is not a spare.

**Readiness: L2, not L3.** The scorecard's L3 for a model worker is "its evaluation set at its bar". For the
Strategist the checkable bar — every live plan passes `checkPlan` — shows a plan is SOUND (cites real evidence,
states no figure the job lacks, no concept twice). It does not show a plan is GOOD. That has no ground truth
until people approve and edit real plans on the review screen (Q-APX3's rule for the judge, applied here), so
L3 is recorded as a gap and not claimed.

Planted cases (8): a sound plan that must pass; an invented evidence id; an angle with no job fact; one concept
twice; a video format nobody can make; an invented pay figure; an added sign-on bonus; two angles only.

Not built: the approval and edit routes, `workers.plan`, the contact-field boundary test (Q-APX2), the plan
review screen, producers reading the plan's angles.

Mutants: 18 planted one at a time over the worker and the planning step, 17 caught on the first pass. The
survivor was the guard for a plan the store refuses after it passed its check, which no real input reaches; it
has its own test file now (`planCampaign.storeRefusal.test.ts`), re-planted and caught.
