**2026-11-15 · Track W · Q-APX6 ruled (b) — planning is mandatory**

The owner: *"planing should be mandetory"*. Until this step a campaign was planned only where the carrier had
switched the Strategist on; anywhere else a draft went straight to making ads.

Built:

- **One road.** The machine's `draft → compose → composing` row is removed, and the `compose` event with it. A
  draft's only move is to `planning`; `composing` is entered only from plan review, on a person's approval that
  names the plan (AX032).
- **The per-carrier choice is removed** (`plansFor`, and its read of the settings). The Strategist's switch no
  longer chooses a road: switched off, the campaign stops at planning with the reason, and a person switches it
  on and presses *Try planning again*.
- **Nothing enters `composing` without a named plan.** Found by a mutant: with the draft's row gone, a campaign
  that failed while planning was still offered *Try making the ads again* (`failed → resume_compose`), which made
  ads with no plan. The machine cannot tell where a campaign failed, so the rule is held once in
  `applyCampaignEvent` for every row that enters `composing`: the campaign names its plan, or the move names it;
  otherwise `no_approved_plan`, recorded as a refusal, with the sentence "Use Try planning again, then approve the
  plan."
- **A host with no planner makes no ad**: a draft is its work only where a planner exists (`agentHasWork`).
- **Settings › Ads control** labels the switch "Plans the campaign — no ad is made without a plan".
- **The end-to-end scenarios** (`packages/ads-golden`) walk the planned road: the harness has a planner, and a
  person's `plan_approved` step names the plan.

Not changed, and why:

- **D-RA9 stands**: the Strategist starts off like every agent (plan §5a has the reasoning).
- **Campaigns made before the ruling** (staging has some; production none): one already composing finishes on
  its seeded draw; one in review can be approved and launched; none can be sent to make ads again unplanned.
- **The database still allows a campaign into `composing` with no plan** from any state but plan review.
  Holding the rule there too is a migration one release after this one.

Mutants: 10 planted one at a time, 10 caught. The first pass caught 4 of 5: an agent row from `draft` into
`composing` put back in the machine passed every test. The test "every row of the machine that enters composing
is a person's, and none leaves draft: the list this rule guards" was written for it, and looking for why it
survived is what found the `failed → resume_compose` hole above.
