**2026-11-15 · Track W · V2c-8 — each plan idea reaches the writers**

Until this step an approved plan decided WHICH kinds of ad were made and in what order, and the words and the
picture still came from the preset's own formula: the idea a person approved never reached the page.

Built:

- **`writerAngle`** (`@silvicom/shared`, `campaignPlan.ts`): the plan's angle with a preset's lead and picture —
  the comparison `planDraw` draws by, read the other way — as two sentences, `idea` and `proof`. `why` stays
  behind: it argues to the person approving and cites results a writer has no use for.
- **The campaign's compose step reads the approved plan once**, hands it to the draw, and hands each creative
  its angle. A campaign whose approved plan is gone stops before any creative. A campaign that was not planned
  (Q-APX6: planning is the carrier's choice) reads no plan and hands no idea.
- **The Copywriter (`copywriter@5`)** is handed `plan: { idea, proof }` or null. With a plan every candidate
  leads with the idea and the candidates differ in how it is said; with none they differ in angle, as before.
  The plan adds no fact: the claim check reads the answer against the brief exactly as before.
- **The Art director (`artDirector@6`)** is handed `idea` or null, never the proof: the proof is about the
  job's facts and the picture carries none (D-AD9).
- **The craft judge and the checker are not told.** The judge's request is built from the Copywriter's message;
  the plan is taken out of it, so its recordings stand and its prompt, which says nothing of a plan, is not
  handed a field it was never told about. Ranking by "does it carry the idea" is a judge change of its own.
- A person's own words for a creative (Q-ADL7) are not rewritten from the idea, and the picture they keep is
  replayed, not redrawn.
