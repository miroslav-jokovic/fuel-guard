  - shared `ads/leadQuality.ts`: the `LeadQuality` interface (injected, the Q-AD34 pattern), the counts, and pure

    `does_not_meet` counts; `unknown` and a missed preference never do. `allPages`/`byIds` are now exported from
    `leadOutcomes.ts`, not copied.

    the uuid column (22P02). The bell is `ads_lead_quality`, keyed per campaign and rule (rung once). It moves
    nothing and spends nothing.

    holds it back.

    "an industry rule of thumb". The form round-trips the group both ways (outcome feed's trap), and a saved
    group equal to the defaults is kept.
