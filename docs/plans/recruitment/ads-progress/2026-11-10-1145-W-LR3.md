**2026-11-10 · Track W · LR3 — a lead with nothing to dial is said on both roads, and rings nobody**

- From `ADS-AGENTS-AUDIT-2026-11-10.md` F-LR9. `alertNoContactDetail` was counted only on leads the BACKFILL stored.
  With the webhook healthy, `leadFetch.ts` stores the lead first and the backfill sees a duplicate, so the alert
  could not ring exactly when intake worked — while `lead_new` and the `lead_uncontacted` alarm both rang for a
  lead nobody can call.
- Built:
  - `ads/leads/leadFetch.ts` rings the same alert (same daily key per connection) when the lead it stored is new
    and the sink says it is not contactable. A duplicate rings nothing: the road that stored it already said so.
  - `recruiting/leads/leadSink.ts` rings `lead_new` only for a lead with a usable phone or an e-mail. The lead is
    still stored, owned, judged and listed.
  - `recruiting/leads/leadUncontacted.ts` leaves such a lead out of the alarm and counts it
    (`no_contact_detail` on the sweep's result). A lead with an e-mail and no phone still rings.
- Golden: `text-aimed-at-a-reader-stays-data.yaml` — its malformed-phone lead has no e-mail either, so it now
  rings no `lead_new` (`rung: [by-backfill]`).
