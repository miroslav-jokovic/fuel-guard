**2026-11-17 · Track W · V4h — a motion ad made from a finished still**

Built:

- `compose/creativeMotion.ts` `makeMotion`: the picture of a still, its words in both languages, the kit and the
  preset in; a motion ad out. In order: the layers and the timeline for each language (they cost nothing, so an ad
  whose words cannot be drawn or timed buys no clip); ONE clip on the render road for both languages; the frame
  check for each language before any file is made; the join; each file stored by its bytes. The answer is the
  record `ads_creatives.motion` will hold (`adMotionSchema`, shared).
- Every stop is the motion ad's only, with a reason `last_error` can carry. The still is not touched.
- A clip more than one frame shorter than asked is refused: the join holds a clip's last frame, so a short clip
  freezes under the words (found in V4f).
- `MOTION_DIRECTION`: the one sentence every clip is asked with — a slow camera push-in, the vehicle and every
  object keep their shape, nothing new appears, no text, no people. OURS. Two of the three candidate models refuse
  a request with no prompt, and no agent has ever been asked for a motion direction.
- `motionClip.ts`: `readAdClip`, `storeAdClip`. `queuedRender`: a clip's job has its own key, so it is not taken
  for the still's job of the same creative, picture and attempt.

Not built: the call from compose (waits for migration 0624 on main); the simulator's video model, so a staging walk
can show a motion ad with no key; the `video` kind in `CAMPAIGN_WORKERS`; the publisher's video road; the player.
