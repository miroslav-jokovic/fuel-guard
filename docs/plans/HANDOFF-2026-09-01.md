## 2. The one paragraph that matters most

**The fuel section's worst problem is not its pages — it is that three of its surfaces carry data
nobody can act on, and one root cause explains most of it.** Per-fill Samsara telematics is fetched as a
*side effect of scoring*, and the bulk path deliberately skips it to protect the rate limit, so **76.8%
of fills have never had telematics fetched at all**. That starves the tank-capacity and sensor-reliability
learners, which is why 101 of 145 trucks carry a capacity that contradicts the one the product already
learned, which is why `cumulative_overfuel` fires 89 times with **55 false positives and zero confirmed**,
which is most of the **2.9%** alert precision.

**And the history is fully recoverable** — verified against the live Samsara API. So the correct order is
**fix collection, re-measure, then decide what to do about the rules** — not the other way round.

## 5. The two live defects, so nobody re-derives them

`GET /webhooks` on the Samsara account returns **two** webhooks. Ours, `Fleetguardweb`:

- **Wrong path.** It posts to `https://fleetguardweb-production.up.railway.app/api/webhooks`. The
  handler is mounted at **`POST /api/webhooks/samsara`** (`app.use("/api/webhooks", webhooksRouter())`
  + `router.post("/samsara", …)`). Every delivery 404s.
- **Wrong events.** It subscribes to `RouteStopArrival`, `RouteStopDeparture`, `RouteStopResequence`,
  `RouteStopEarlyLateArrival`, `RouteStopEtaUpdated`. `processSamsaraWebhook` is written for a
  fuel-level drop and writes `fuel_events`.

Either alone explains `fuel_events` = **0 rows, no event ever received**.

The other webhook is `Fleetpal Webhook` → a third-party URL, events `VehicleCreated`, `VehicleUpdated`,
`DvirSubmitted`. **Do not touch it** (Q-SAM3).

## 7. Traps this work already hit

- ⚠ **Parallel chats share this working tree.** Mid-session another chat switched the tree to its own
  branch *and had committed its work on top of mine*. **Always `git branch --show-current` before
  committing, branch from `origin/main` explicitly, and prefer `git worktree add` for anything
  long-running.** Both plans were written in scratchpad worktrees for this reason.
- **`fuel_business_date()` / `fuel_station_tz()` (0247) carry `set search_path`**, which blocks SQL
  inlining — the ~128×-per-row scalar penalty that took the spend page down before. Bounded sets only.
  They are `stable`, not `immutable`, so they cannot back a generated column or index expression.
  FUEL-T1 therefore uses a **trigger**.
- **`scripts/table-writers.json` lists 11 writers of `fuel_transactions`, including the browser.** Any
  derived column must be database-maintained, not stamped at call sites.
- **0252's D-AG1 — "THIS SUMS. IT DOES NOT DERIVE."** The MPG plausibility band and `robustWindowMiles`
  are judgement and must stay in TS. FUEL-T3 is split into T3a (sums, ships) and T3b (a spike).
- **`check-section-policies.mjs` and `routeGates.test.ts` cannot catch a wrong role list** — one checks
  migrations above 0260, the other only that a gate *exists*. FUEL-T2 extends the latter.
- **`pnpm lint` scans `.claude/worktrees`** — filter the path before believing a failure count.
- **A fresh worktree needs** `pnpm install`, `pnpm build:rn`, and the two `.env` files copied, or ~14
  suites fail for reasons that are not your change.
- **A merge is served ~9 minutes before its migration is applied** — a column and its first reader ship
  in two merges (`lint:migration-ordering`).

## 8. Method, which is not negotiable on this work

Everything in both plans was written from measurement. Three steps changed shape *after* checking they
were buildable (FUEL-T1's trigger, FUEL-T2's scope, FUEL-T3's split), and two are recorded as
assumptions rather than dressed as facts (FUEL-T3b's seam, SAM-S6's expected benefit). **Keep that.**

- Measure before claiming. State deviations. Prove a test can fail.
- One step per PR, branched from `origin/main` explicitly, CI green, merge commit.
- When a step ships, mark it **DONE** in its plan with "What shipped" and "Verified by:" naming the
  gates. When a question is answered, strike it through **in place** with the answer and the date.
- **The plan is the memory between sessions.** This handoff is not; it goes stale.
