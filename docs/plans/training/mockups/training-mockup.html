The driver's link

What a driver sees after tapping the text. Try the signal switch:

  • Target: the page opens in about 2 seconds on 3G. It has no web font and nothing to install, and the budget is under 100 KB before the video starts. This is a target, not yet measured.
  • Each video comes in three sizes. On weak signal it plays the 360p one: 2.1 MB for a whole 3½ minute film, measured on film 01. The writing stays readable.
  • The next chapter downloads while this one plays, so a dead spot does not stop the picture.
  • It remembers where they stopped. Tap the link again tomorrow and it starts at the same second.
  • Answers are kept on the phone with no signal and sent when signal comes back.
  • No skipping ahead on the first watch. After that, any chapter can be watched again.
